Set your watch, measure its daily rate, and configure desktop widgets.

Getting started

  • Requires macOS 14 or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  • One purchase includes every feature. No account, subscription, or in-app purchase.
  • Internet is needed for PTB reference-time checks. Audio analysis and lunar calculations run locally.

Set your watch

  1. Launch Watch Setting Bench. The setting bench shows the time, date, calendar, moon phase, and GMT locations.
  2. Wait for the reference-time status to show a successful check. Open the reference-time details to inspect the source, last check, correction, and uncertainty. Use Check reference time to retry.
  3. Use the seconds display to align your watch. The app does not set your watch or change the Mac’s system clock.
  4. Open Manage locations to add or reorder cities. In Settings, choose a reference city or follow your Mac’s time zone.

PTB synchronization is a reference-time estimate over the network. Network delay and display timing affect the practical result; the status panel shows the current quality. If a fresh check is unavailable, check the displayed source before setting your watch.

Measure an automatic watch’s accuracy

  1. Open Accuracy (or press ⌘3). Choose a microphone from the input menu.
  2. Place a running automatic or hand-wound watch close to the microphone in a quiet room. Keep the watch still and avoid touching the microphone.
  3. Press Start listening and allow microphone access. Watch the waveform and confirmed beat markers. The app detects the nominal beat rate automatically.
  4. Once a steady beat is found, the daily rate appears in seconds per day, together with statistical uncertainty, beat rate in bph, balance frequency in Hz, accepted beat count, and elapsed measurement time.
  5. Let the measurement continue to collect more usable beats. The history shows how the estimate develops. Press Stop when finished.
  6. Alternatively, choose Open audio and select a recording. The file is analyzed locally.

A clean signal usually yields an initial estimate after about five seconds. More usable audio can reduce statistical uncertainty; noise, weak ticks, changing rates, and gaps can limit or interrupt that improvement. The app holds the last estimate while reacquiring a lost signal.

Understand the result: statistical uncertainty describes detected beat timing. It does not include an unknown microphone or recording sample-clock error. The PTB time reference does not calibrate the audio clock. If you have an independently measured correction, enter it under Audio clock calibration before the next measurement. Changing microphones resets the correction.

This feature estimates rate from sound. It does not measure amplitude or beat error and is not a substitute for a calibrated professional timegrapher. Quartz watches and recordings without a clear mechanical escapement beat may not produce a result.

Add desktop widgets

  1. Open the app at least once.
  2. Right-click the Mac desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  3. Search for Watch Setting Bench and add Atomic Time, Moon Phase, GMT Register, or Settings Bench.
  4. Open Your Widgets in the app to preview the widgets and choose a theme. Widget changes appear on their next macOS-managed refresh.

Atomic Time and Moon Phase are small widgets; GMT Register is medium; Settings Bench is large. Click a widget to open the related app view. macOS controls refresh frequency and can dim or tint widgets depending on desktop settings.

Themes, appearance, and languages

Choose among ten widget themes in Your Widgets: Atelier, Monochrome, Blueprint, Rally, Nocturne, Bauhaus, Art Deco, Deep Sea, Dune, and Terminal. Settings offers light, dark, or system appearance, regional date and hour formats, and a mirrored moon disc.

The app supports English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish, Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Choose a language in Settings or follow the system language.

Moon and calendar details

Open the moon panel for the current phase, illumination, age, and upcoming lunar events. Use the disc explorer to preview the moon display, then choose Return to today to restore the current phase. Calculations are performed on your Mac. Open the calendar and GMT panels for additional setting information.

Troubleshooting

  • No waveform: check the selected microphone, its input level, connection, and macOS microphone permission. Press Stop and start a new measurement after an input change.
  • No stable rate: move the watch closer, reduce background noise, and use a longer recording. Avoid clipping, speech, music, and handling noise. Some microphones cannot pick up quiet watch ticks clearly.
  • Unexpected offset: check audio-clock calibration and recording quality. A small sample-rate error can materially change a seconds-per-day estimate.
  • Reference time unavailable: check the internet connection and retry. Some networks may block the PTB WebSocket service. The clock source indicator explains the fallback state.
  • Widget missing or stale: launch the app, confirm macOS 14 or later, and remove and re-add the widget. Allow macOS time to refresh it.

Features are the same in all regions. Language, regional formats, local time zone, daylight-saving rules, and network access can affect what is displayed. No login credentials or special account setup are required.
