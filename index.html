WATCH SETTING BENCH FOR MAC

Privacy Policy

How Watch Setting Bench handles audio, preferences, reference-time requests, and support information.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • Watch audio is analyzed on your Mac. It is not uploaded or saved by the app.
  • No account, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.
  • Reference time is requested directly from PTB over an encrypted connection.
  • Apple handles the one-time App Store purchase. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to Watch Setting Bench for macOS and this privacy and support website, maintained by Potato Planet Studios (potatoplaneta). The app helps you set mechanical watches, view time and lunar information, and estimate a watch’s daily rate from its sound.

We do not operate an app backend, sell personal data, use advertising identifiers, or include third-party tracking or analytics services. No app account is required.

2. Microphone and selected recordings

Microphone access is requested only when you start listening on the Accuracy page. The app uses your selected input to detect watch beats and calculate a rate estimate locally. Raw audio is processed in bounded memory buffers; the app does not save microphone recordings or transmit audio. A short waveform display and beat timing results remain in memory during the measurement session.

If you choose Open audio, macOS grants read access to that selected file. It is decoded and analyzed on your Mac. The app does not change the original recording, upload it, or retain a copy. The file name appears in the current view. Rate history and manual audio-clock calibration are session data, not a cloud record.

Listening stops when you press Stop, leave the Accuracy page, quit the app, or the Mac sleeps. macOS shows its microphone indicator while the microphone is active.

3. Data stored on your Mac

The app stores your display preferences, language, date and hour formats, selected city time zones, widget theme, moon orientation, and the latest reference-time check locally. The app and its widgets share this information through a macOS app-group container. A time check includes timing measurements and the reference clock’s status; it does not contain audio.

City selections are made from an offline catalog. The app reads the Mac’s configured time zone, and does not request GPS or Location Services permission. Lunar calculations run locally using the bundled Astronomy Engine. There is no developer-operated cloud synchronization.

4. PTB reference-time service

The app automatically checks reference time with the Physikalisch-Technische Bundesanstalt (PTB), Germany’s national metrology institute, and also checks when you request a refresh. It connects to wss://uhr.ptb.de/time using TLS. PTB and the network infrastructure receive your IP address and ordinary connection metadata needed to serve the request, plus a randomly generated echo token used to match timing responses. The app does not send your audio, selected cities, watch measurements, email address, or a persistent user identifier to PTB.

PTB operates its own service and determines its server logging and retention. See PTB’s privacy information. The developer does not receive these requests. The app uses an ephemeral network session without browser cookies. If the service cannot be reached, the app indicates the reference status and can use the Mac’s clock. Closing the app stops its time checks; widgets read locally shared data.

5. App Store purchases

Watch Setting Bench is a one-time paid download. All features are included; there are no subscriptions, trials, in-app purchases, or paid accounts inside the app. Apple processes payment and delivery. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide aggregated sales reports or diagnostics under its services and your Apple privacy choices. See Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Retention, deletion, and permissions

Settings remain locally until you change or remove them. Uninstalling the app may leave preferences and shared widget data. To remove them, quit the app, remove its widgets, and delete bench-v1.json and any recovery copy from the group container ~/Library/Group Containers/33JMZXFWB9.com.PotatoPlanetStudios.WatchSettingBench.shared/. Contact us if you need help. Your backups may retain copies until you remove them through your backup provider.

Manage microphone permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Declining microphone access does not prevent use of the setting bench, widgets, lunar information, or selected audio files. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data held only on your Mac.

7. This website and support

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, external fonts, or contact forms. GitHub receives information required to serve pages, including IP addresses and request metadata, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond and troubleshoot. Correspondence remains in the support mailbox until deleted; email us to request removal of messages we control. Our Gmail mailbox is provided by Google under its privacy policy. Email services may retain their own copies.

GitHub issue content and usernames are public and follow GitHub’s retention rules. Share only what is needed to explain your issue. Audio is never sent automatically; if you choose to send a sample for support, first check that it contains no private conversations.

8. Policy changes

We may update this page when the app or its privacy practices change. The current policy and its update date will remain available here.
